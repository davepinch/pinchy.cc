  **Barbara Jill Walters** (25 September 1929 – 30 December 2022) was an American broadcast journalist, author, and television personality. Known for her interviewing ability and popularity with viewers, Walters appeared as the host of numerous television programs, including *Today*, *The View*, *20/20*, and the *ABC Evening News*. Walters was a working journalist from 1951 until 2015.
