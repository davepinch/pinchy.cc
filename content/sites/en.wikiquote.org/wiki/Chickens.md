  **Chickens** (Gallus gallus domesticus) are a domesticated red junglefowl who appeared the most common and widespread domestic animals, with a population of more than 19 billion in 2011, there are more chickens in the world than any other species of bird or domestic animal. Humans keep chickens primarily as a source of food, consuming both their meat and their eggs.
