  **Christopher Asher Wray** (17 December 1966) is an American lawyer serving as the 8th Director of the Federal Bureau of Investigation under Presidents Donald Trump and Joe Biden. From 2003 to 2005 Wray served as Assistant Attorney General in charge of the Criminal Division in the George W. Bush Administration. From 2005 to 2016 he was a litigation partner with the law firm King & Spalding.
