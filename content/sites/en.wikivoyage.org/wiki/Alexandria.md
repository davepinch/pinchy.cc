  **Alexandria** (pronounced *al-ig-ZAN-dree-uh* ; Arabic: الإسكندرية ‎/ اسكندرية *al-Iskandariyya* / *Eskendereyya* ) is Egypt's second largest city (5.2 million people in 2018), its largest seaport, and the country's window onto the Mediterranean Sea. The city is a faded shadow of its former glorious cosmopolitan self, but still worth a visit for its many cultural attractions – particularly the new Library of Alexandria – and still-palpable glimpses of its past.
