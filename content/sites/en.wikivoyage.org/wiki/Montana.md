  **Montana**, known as *Big Sky Country*, is a state of contrasts, from the eastern plains to the towering peaks of the Rocky Mountains in the west. It is the fourth largest of the United States but one of the least populated, making for wide open spaces, lonesome highways and dramatic scenery, both to the east and west of the Continental Divide.
