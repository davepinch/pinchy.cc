  **Rainier** is a town in the Northern Oregon Coast region. It is a charming, small town on the Colombia River where colonial history comes alive and visitors enjoy the waterfront views. It is a stop on the Lewis and Clark Trail for travelers interested in the historic exploration of America's northwest region.
