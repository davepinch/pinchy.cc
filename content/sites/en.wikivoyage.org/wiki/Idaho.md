  **Idaho** is a state in the Rocky Mountains of the United States. Idaho is known for its wilderness; there are snow-capped mountains that reach 10,000 to 12,500 feet (3,000 to 3,800 m) above sea level. One of the whitewater rivers runs through the country's deepest river canyon. Most of the land north of Boise is national forest or state forest.
