excerpt: >-
  **Moscow** is a city in North Central Idaho with a population of around 25,000 people (2020), an agricultural center and the home of the University of Idaho. The surrounding area is generally referred to as the Moscow-Pullman area -- Pullman is a city in Washington, that is fairly symbiotic with Moscow. They are 8 mi (13 km) apart.
