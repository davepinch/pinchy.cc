  Pacific Northwest novelist Tom Robbins, profoundly provoked and inspired by what he called the "1960s renaissance," is often hailed as a comic/spiritual chronicler of that tumultuous decade. But his nine novels and numerous writings spanning a career of more than 40 years place him in a broader perspective as a futurist, a sharp-eyed observer of American aesthetics, and fanciful cultural gadfly.
