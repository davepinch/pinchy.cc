  **KKs 3** is an isolated dwarf galaxy near the Local Group. It is unusual because it is gas poor and very isolated in the halo of the local group. KKs 3 is about 7 million light years away from Earth. It is categorised as a dwarf spheroidal dSph galaxy. The mass of KKs 3 is 2.3 × 107M☉ (23 million times the mass of the Sun) with a blue absolute magnitude of −10.8. Three-quarters of its stars are over 12 billion years old. Coordinates are R.A. = 02h 24m 44.4s, Dec. = −73°30′51".
