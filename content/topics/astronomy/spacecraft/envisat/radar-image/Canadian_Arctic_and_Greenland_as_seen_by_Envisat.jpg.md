description: >-
  Radar image showing the tip of Ellesmere Island (left) in the Canadian Arctic and northwestern Greenland (right). The light area to the right is the ice-covered surface of the Greenland Ice Sheet, while the darker stream extending across the lower centre of the image is the northern hemisphere’s longest floating glacier, Petermann. The image was made from Envisat's Advanced Synthetic Aperture Radar (ASAR) data acquired in 2008.
greenland ice sheet: >-
  The [Greenland Ice Sheet](/greenland-ice-sheet/) is the fluffy white area in the lower-right (southeast) part of the image. This sheet feeds the [Petermann Glacier](/petermann-glacier/), which is the dark stream near the bottom of the image.

petermann glacier: >-
  The [Petermann Glacier](/petermann-glacier/) is the dark stream that runs westward from the white [Greenland Ice Sheet](/greenland-ice-sheet/). The glacier follows the [Petermann Fjord](/petermann-fjord) until it exits into the [Hall Basin](/hall-basin/).
