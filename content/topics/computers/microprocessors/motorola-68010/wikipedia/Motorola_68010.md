excerpt: >-
  The **Motorola MC68010** and Motorola MC68012 (pronounced "sixty-eight-oh-ten" and "sixty-eight-oh-twelve" respectively) are 16/32-bit microprocessors from Motorola, released in 1982 as successors to the Motorola 68000. The 68010 and 68012 added virtualization features, optimized loops and fixed several small flaws to the 68000. The MC68010 variants were pin compatible with its predecessor while the MC68012 is an 84-pin PGA version with its directly accessible memory space extended to 2 GiB.
