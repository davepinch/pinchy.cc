  At 6:45 a.m. on May 13th Linda Holden Givens passes away with her daughter by her side unexpectedly to cancer. She leaves behind her only biological daughter Alegra. Linda attended Franklin High school graduated class of 74'.She proudly attended the University of Washington. Of course this was not enough, Linda continued her education by getting her masters. A lover of history, the human experience and genetics, she used this to write two books about her family. She loved engaging in others history helping them find there roots at the same time. She loved being active and outside, walking and running in the woods smelling the mesmerizing Washington air.
