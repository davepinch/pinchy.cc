  **Rachel Anne Accurso** (née Griffin; born November 30, 1982), better known as Ms. Rachel, is an American YouTuber, educator, singer-songwriter, and activist. She created the eponymous YouTube series Ms. Rachel (originally Songs for Littles), which combines original songs and classic children's music with a focus on early language development for infants, toddlers, and preschoolers; as of 2026 the channel has more than 20 million subscribers and over 16 billion views.
