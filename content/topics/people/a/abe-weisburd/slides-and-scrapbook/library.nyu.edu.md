  Abe Weisburd (1907-1995) was a radical activist and journalist who wrote regularly for the National Guardian (aka Guardian), a leftist weekly newspaper (1948-1992). This collection contains color slides and a scrapbook, which primarily date from the 1970s and early 1980s. The slides incorporate both his private travel slides and his political travel slides spanning the years 1969-1984. The scrapbook contains writings by Weisburd, biographical notes, photos, and letters from friends and family.
