  With an enthusiastic smile and expressive brown eyes, Sean Margetis entered into eternal rest with his Heavenly Father on July 1, 2014, after a tragic swimming accident at Moulton Falls. Sean enjoyed learning and received his Associate degree at Clark College after graduating from Hudson's Bay High School. Sean was preceded in death by his father (Nick) and mother (Diane). He leaves behind his fiancé, Heidi Close; and Sharon Brown, who he called "Mom". He was loved and cherished by both. His Celebration of Life will be held at 1:00 p.m., Saturday, July 19, 2014 at Walnut Grove Church, 6004 NE 72nd Ave., Vancouver 98661. In lieu of flowers, donations may be made to Freedom House, PO Box 33510, Portland, OR 97292.
