  **Leroy Eldridge Cleaver** (August 31, 1935 – May 1, 1998) was an African American writer and political activist, fashion designer, self-confessed serial rapist, and an early leader of the Black Panther Party serving as Minister of Information, and while in exile, Head of the International Section of the Panthers. As editor of the official Panthers' newspaper, The Black Panther, Cleaver's influence on the direction of the party was rivaled only by founders Huey P. Newton and Bobby Seale.
