  **Hamlin Park** is an 80-acre (32.4-hectare) public park in Shoreline, Washington, in the United States. The park includes a wooded area with trails, a softball/baseball field with bleachers, two playgrounds, and a picnic shelter. An orienteering course with waypoint markers is laid out within the park.
