  **Chattaroy** is an unincorporated community in Spokane County, Washington, United States. The town is located on U.S. Route 2 approximately 10 miles (16 km) north-northeast of Spokane at the confluence of the Little Spokane River and Deer Creek. Today, Chattaroy Elementary School is a school that teaches Preschool to fourth grade and is in the Riverside School District.
