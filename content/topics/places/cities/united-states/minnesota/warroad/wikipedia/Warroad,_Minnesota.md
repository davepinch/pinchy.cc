  **Warroad** is a city in Roseau County, Minnesota, United States, at the southwest corner of Lake of the Woods, 7.5 miles (12.1 km) south of Canada. The population was 1,830 at the 2020 census. Warroad had its own newspaper before it was incorporated in 1901.
