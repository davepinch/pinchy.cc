  **Pataha** is a small, unincorporated community and census designated place (CDP) in Garfield County, Washington, United States. It is located about 4 miles (6.4 km) east of Pomeroy, the Garfield County seat. As of the 2020 census, Pataha had a population of 106. Pataha is home to the historic Houser Mill, a working water-powered flour mill originally built in 1879.
