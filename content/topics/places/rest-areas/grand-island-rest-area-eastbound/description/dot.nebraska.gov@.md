title: "description of Grand Island Rest Area Eastbound (dot.nebraska.gov)"
✂️: >-
  Located 4 miles west of the Phillips Interchange. Contains 23.5 acres with parking for 7 trucks and 26 cars. The area has 7 picnic shelters, 2 picnic tables, children’s play area, family/alternate restroom, and a summer tourist information station. This area also includes a small lake for public fishing. *(Erma’s Desire: By John Raimondi)
