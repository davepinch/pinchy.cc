  **Columbia Hills Historical State Park** is a Washington public recreation area located 6 miles (9.7 km) east of Dallesport on SR 14 in Klickitat County. The state park occupies 3,338 acres (1,351 ha) on Horsethief Lake, an impoundment of the Columbia River. It was created in 2003 with the merger of Horsethief Lake State Park and Dalles Mountain Ranch.
