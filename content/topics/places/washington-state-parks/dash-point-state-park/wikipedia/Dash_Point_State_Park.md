  **Dash Point State Park** is a 398-acre (161 ha) Washington public recreation area on Puget Sound that straddles the line between King and Pierce counties. The state park has over 3,300 feet (1,000 m) of shoreline, 140 campsites, 11 miles of trails for hiking and mountain biking, and offers beachcombing, fishing, swimming, birdwatching, windsurfing, skimboarding, and wildlife viewing.
