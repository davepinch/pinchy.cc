Many Masters from India, who travel through the U.S.A, initiating chelas, make promise of taking on the individual's karma. It could be remarked that this is not one step removed from the popular misconceptions of Jesus Christ as a bearer of others' sins! Since money conveys great worth to the materialistic West, Masters who charge huge sums for their lessons or courses rank high on the list of sought-after leaders.
