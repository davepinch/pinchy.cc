  This period is one of increasing liberation for women and will continue to be so. Why is this? Because a true emergence of the woman soul on the world scene is imperative if a balance of the sexes is to be achieved. Each sex is the balance of the other, a half of the whole, an expression of the one androgenous being created in the beginning by God. For ages woman has been held in subjection to animal-man whereas god-man knows her to be his equal and his companion and would never place her beneath him.
