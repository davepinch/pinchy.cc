  I write this editorial in the week of Robert Kennedy’s assasination. Martin Luther King, exponent of non-violence, has also been assasinated since our last issue. Anger, violence, sadism, murder, and perversion are the fruits of this sick civilization which exists, not only in America but over most of the world.
