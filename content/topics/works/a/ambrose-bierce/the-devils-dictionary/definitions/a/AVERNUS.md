  Facilis descensus Averni,
  The poet remarks; and the sense
  Of it is that when down-hill I turn I

  *Jehal Dai Lupe*
quote: "**AVERNUS**, **n.** The lake by which the ancients entered the infernal regions. The fact that access to the infernal regions was obtained by a lake is believed by the learned Marcus Ansello Scrutator to have suggested the Christian rite of baptism by immersion. This, however, has been shown by Lactantius to be an error."
